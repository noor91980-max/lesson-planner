Automatic Lesson Plan Generator

How to Use the Tool for the First Time

Quick guide: what to enter, where to get the information, and what the tool will generate.

Before you start: Open the approved course specification and the weekly content plan, then prepare today’s lesson title and duration. You do not need to write a separate quality report; the tool generates the quality page automatically from the same data.
1

Lesson Information

What do you enter? Course name, week, lesson title, duration, and delivery mode.

Where do I get it from?
Course plan / weekly schedule + class timetable + the topic assigned for the week.
2

Learning Outcome Code

What do you select? A code such as K1, K2, S1, S2, V1… then a suitable action verb and the outcome topic.

Where do I get the code?
From the course learning outcomes table in the approved course specification. Do not invent a new code.
3

Activity & Assessment

What do you select? The learning activity, assessment method, target, and platform/tool used.

How do I choose them?
Choose what matches the outcome level: remember/understand → short questions; apply/analyze → case or practical activity.
4

Lesson Delivery Plan

What do you select? Lesson stages and the time allocated to each stage.

How do I choose them?
Based on the actual class duration. Make the total planned minutes match the lesson duration as closely as possible.
What is the final output?
عند الضغط على «Preview» أو «PDF» تنتج الأداة تلقائيًا: Lesson Plan Card + Learning Outcomes & Assessment Matrix + Lesson Delivery Plan الزمنية + Automatic Completion Score للجودة + Approval & Quality Page showing completed items, missing items, and the final recommendation. The instructor does not need to write the quality page manually.
